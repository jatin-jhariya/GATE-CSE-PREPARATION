# PROFIT & LOSS — GATE

## 1. BASIC FORMULAS

`Profit = SP − CP`

`Loss = CP − SP`

`Profit% = (Profit/CP) × 100`

`Loss% = (Loss/CP) × 100`

### Direct relation

Profit `p%`:

`SP = CP(1 + p/100)`

Loss `p%`:

`SP = CP(1 − p/100)`

---

## 2. CP ↔ SP

If profit = `p%`:

`SP : CP = (100+p) : 100`

If loss = `p%`:

`SP : CP = (100−p) : 100`

---

## 3. SUCCESSIVE PROFIT / LOSS 

Two successive changes `a%` and `b%`:

`Net % = a + b + ab/100`

Use signs:

Profit → `+`

Loss → `−`

Example:

20% profit → 10% loss

`Net = 20 − 10 − 2 = 8% profit`

---

## 4. SAME % PROFIT & LOSS

If an article is sold at `x%` profit and another at `x%` loss:

Overall result:

`Loss% = x²/100`

**Important:** CPs must be equal.

---

## 5. MARKED PRICE & DISCOUNT

`Discount = MP − SP`

`Discount% = (Discount/MP) × 100`

Therefore:

`SP = MP(1 − d/100)`

---

## 6. SUCCESSIVE DISCOUNTS 

For discounts `a%` and `b%`:

`Equivalent discount = a + b − ab/100`

Example:

20%, 10%

`= 20 + 10 − 2 = 28%`

---

## 7. PROFIT AFTER DISCOUNT

If discount = `d%` and desired profit = `p%`:

`MP/CP = (100+p)/(100-d)`

Hence:

`Markup% = [p+d+(pd/100)]`

---

## 8. DISCOUNT + PROFIT

If MP is `x%` above CP and discount is `d%`:

`SP/CP = (1+x/100)(1-d/100)`

Compare SP with CP to get profit/loss.

---

## 9. FALSE WEIGHT 

If seller gives `x%` less quantity but charges for full quantity:

`Gain% = x/(100−x) × 100`

If actual weight = `w`, claimed weight = `W`:

`Gain% = (W−w)/w × 100`

---

## 10. TWO ARTICLES — SAME SP 

One at `x%` profit and another at `x%` loss:

`Overall Loss% = x²/100`

**Only when CPs are equal?**
For same SP, the CPs are NOT equal.

Correct general result:

`Overall Loss% = x²/100`

---

## 11. RECOVERY AFTER LOSS

After loss of `x%`, required gain to return to original CP:

`Required gain% = x/(100−x) × 100`

Example:

20% loss → required gain

`20/80 × 100 = 25%`

---

## 12. RATIO METHOD 

Profit `p%`:

`CP : SP = 100 : (100+p)`

Loss `p%`:

`CP : SP = 100 : (100−p)`

Useful when **multiple quantities/articles** are involved.

---

# GATE QUICK TRICKS

`Profit/Loss % → always on CP`

`Discount % → always on MP`

`Successive changes → a+b+ab/100`

`Successive discounts → a+b−ab/100`

`Loss x% → recovery = x/(100−x) ×100`

`False weight → gain on actual quantity`

`Same CP + ±x% → net loss = x²/100`

`Same SP + ±x% → net loss = x²/100`
